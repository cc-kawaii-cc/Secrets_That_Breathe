Low polygon kraft document envelopes

KraftEnvelopes_MessyStack.prefab: five envelopes, two unfolded flaps,
irregular positions and rotations, 260 triangles total.
KraftEnvelope_Closed.prefab: single closed envelope, 12 triangles.
KraftEnvelope_Open.prefab: single open envelope, 112 triangles.

Each prefab uses one MeshRenderer and one URP material with a BoxCollider.
Scale is (1,1,1), pivot at floor level. Single envelope body: 23.5 x 32.5 cm.
The supplied second image is used directly, with UV mapping that excludes
the background and maps the body/flap separately for the open version.
Paper texture, folded seams and stains come from the supplied photograph.
No white paper contents. Folded flap geometry is fixed, not animated.

Portable FBX files and normalized Unity meshes are in Models.
Editable Blender source, render preview and verification are in ArtSource/KraftEnvelopes.
