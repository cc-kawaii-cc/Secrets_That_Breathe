A4 printed document props

A4Document_Single.prefab: one sheet, 12 triangles.
A4Documents_Stack10.prefab: ten slightly offset/rotated sheets, 120 triangles.
A4Documents_Stack20.prefab: twenty slightly offset/rotated sheets, 240 triangles.

A4 size: 210 x 297 mm. Sheet thickness: 0.18 mm.
Each prefab uses one renderer, two URP materials and a BoxCollider.
Scale is (1,1,1), floor-level pivot. Back and edges are blank white.
The supplied image is used directly as the front print with its aspect ratio
preserved within the A4 sheet. Texture wrap is Clamp for white margins.
Print detail is limited by the supplied reference image resolution.

Portable FBX models and normalized Unity meshes are in Models.
Editable Blender source and preview: ArtSource/A4Documents.
