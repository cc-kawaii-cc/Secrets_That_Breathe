# FD V.2 — full marked lens areas on car texture UVs

Backside leak correction: the original contour projection also marked hidden inner headlamp/wheel-arch surfaces along the same projection rays. The builder now creates separate visibility depth maps for both sides and both ends, and requires both frontmost visibility (3 mm tolerance) and an outward-facing triangle. UV padding is clipped to eligible surfaces so it cannot reintroduce glow behind the lens. The material's current HDR/color settings are preserved. `Emission_before_backside_fix.png` backs up the previous map. `side_before_texture.png` and `side_after_texture.png` compare emission-only output from the reported angle; `backside_fix_validation.json` measures the removed wheel-arch patch. These are CPU geometry/UV diagnostics, not live Unity captures.

Current method, replacing the standalone emitter geometry at the user's request. `FD.prefab` uses `Material/FD_Lights_Emission.mat` on the original Body atlas slot and original Light renderer. `Texture/FD_Lights_Emission.png` shares the original car atlas UV coordinates.

The front map covers the full headlamp interior shown in the user's cyan annotation, in white, mirrored to the other side. It is not limited to the round bulb and does not draw a DRL strip. The rear map covers the full upper lens section marked red, leaving the clear lower section dark, on both sides. Lens texture luminance modulates the emission slightly to retain reflector detail. HDR Emission Color is (3,3,3). Source Base Map, normal/lighting properties, geometry, transforms, pivots and all original IDs are preserved.

The four added emitter objects and their root child entries have been removed. The original prefab structure is restored, apart from the two intended material references. There are 8 mesh objects and 30,624 source-FBX triangles, with no added geometry. The original diffuse car texture is unchanged: an Emission Map is used to make those UV regions emit light, rather than painting bright diffuse pixels and calling them emission.

Source: `../build_full_lens_texture.py`. The housing contours are traced in known diagnostic camera projections and evaluated at the actual FBX triangle/UV positions. `verify.py` checks exact prefab preservation apart from material binding, original source/importer/base texture preservation, map colors and references. `final_validation.json` records checks. `../preview_fd_lights.py` renders diagnostic close/oblique views under `../Lights/`; these use actual source geometry and the saved map, with approximate diffuse + emission and no Bloom. They are not Unity scene captures.

The earlier independent meshes/materials and historical work are retained outside Assets for recovery. No scene file was saved and no screen control was used. Live Unity import/Bloom and standalone builds were not verified in this pass.
